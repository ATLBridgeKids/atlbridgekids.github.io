Signup review

Paste in your CSV (from the Google Sheet or download from a Form response tab), and this will flag likely duplicates and give you a cleaned version to paste back.

This page is not linked from the public site. Only people who know the URL can find it. Don't share the URL with families.

1 · Paste CSV

Expected columns (any order, headers required): timestamp, participant_name, age, graduation_year, email, phone, parent_name, event_id, bridge_level, school, share. Missing columns are OK.

2 · Possible duplicates

Rows grouped by matching name+email, name+phone, or same email across different names. Review each group and delete the row you don't want inside your Google Sheet.

3 · Cleaned signup list (with computed grade)

Grade is auto-calculated from graduation_year using today's date. Newest row wins if a row is a clear duplicate.
