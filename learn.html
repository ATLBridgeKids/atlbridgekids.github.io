Resources

Learning bridge

A few places to practice, learn, and play beyond our in-person sessions.

Jump Start Bridge

A website with lots of information about youth bridge, including teaching material and getting-started guides.

Tricky Bridge

An offline app with a series of mini 5-minute lessons and chances to refine basic bridge skills without needing the internet. My kids like this one.

Bridge Base Online

By far the most popular website and app for playing bridge online, typically with over 10,000 players online. Create a username, join random games, or play with people you know. My username is dredog03.

American Contract Bridge League (ACBL)

The sanctioning organization for bridge clubs and tournaments in the US and Canada.
